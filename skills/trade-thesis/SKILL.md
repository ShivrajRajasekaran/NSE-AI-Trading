# Investment Thesis Builder — KARTHIK

## Trigger
`/trade thesis <TICKER>`

## What To Do

Build a complete investment thesis with conviction levels.

## Output Must Include

1. **Bull Case** — 3 catalysts with probability estimate
   - Catalyst 1: [description] — probability [X]%
   - Catalyst 2: [description] — probability [X]%
   - Catalyst 3: [description] — probability [X]%

2. **Bear Case** — 3 risks with probability estimate
   - Risk 1: [description] — probability [X]%
   - Risk 2: [description] — probability [X]%
   - Risk 3: [description] — probability [X]%

3. **Base Case Target** — 12-month price target with rationale

4. **Entry Strategy**:
   - Immediate entry level OR wait for specific level/condition
   - Staged entry plan (50%/30%/20%)

5. **Exit Plan**:
   - Target 1, Target 2, Target 3
   - Stop loss (hard stop)
   - Trailing stop methodology
   - Time-based exit if thesis doesn't play out

6. **Position Sizing Recommendation** — % of portfolio given conviction

7. **Timeline & Catalysts Calendar**:
   - Near-term (1-3 months): [events]
   - Medium-term (3-6 months): [events]
   - Long-term (6-12 months): [events]

8. **Comparable Companies / Precedents** — who did this before, what happened

9. **Thesis Invalidation** — specific condition that kills the thesis entirely

## Rules
- Be specific — no vague "good company, will do well"
- Assign probabilities — this forces intellectual honesty
- Include downside scenario with specific price level
- Reference real data (earnings, order book, industry trends)
