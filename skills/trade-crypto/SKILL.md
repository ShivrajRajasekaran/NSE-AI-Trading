# Crypto Trading Analysis — KARTHIK

## Trigger
`/trade crypto <COIN>`

## What To Do

Full crypto analysis for Indian traders. Fetch data via CoinGecko, CoinMarketCap, Coinglass.

## Output Must Include

1. **Spot Price** + 24h/7d/30d performance
2. **BTC Dominance Context** — if altcoin, is BTC.D rising (bad for alts) or falling (altseason)
3. **On-Chain Metrics** — exchange inflows/outflows, whale movements
4. **Funding Rate + OI Trend** — crowded longs or shorts?
5. **Liquidation Clusters** — from Coinglass, where are clustered liquidations
6. **Fear & Greed Index** — <25 extreme fear (buy zone), >75 greed (reduce)
7. **Technical Analysis**:
   - Support/Resistance levels
   - Key MAs (21, 50, 200 EMA)
   - Volume analysis (OBV, CVD)
   - RSI, MACD
   - Fibonacci levels
8. **Indian Exchange Availability** — CoinDCX, WazirX, Coindcek
9. **Tax Implication Reminder** — 30% flat tax + 1% TDS on trades >₹50,000
10. **Trade Plan**:
    - Entry, Stop, Target
    - Leverage recommendation (2–3x max for Indian retail)
    - Hold period
    - Invalidation

## Crypto Market Structure
- BTC dominance thresholds: 50%, 55%, 60%
- Altcoin seasons lag BTC by 2-6 weeks after major rallies
- Funding rate + price divergence = potential squeeze
- BTC halving cycles: rally 12-18 months post-halving
- CME futures chart gaps — institutions use these

## Crypto Risk Rules
- Never >5% of portfolio in any single altcoin
- BTC/ETH: max 20-30% each
- Leverage: absolute max 3x swing, 5x intraday
- Stablecoin allocation 20-30% in bear phases
- Hardware wallet for holds >30 days
