# Sector Rotation Analysis — KARTHIK

## Trigger
`/trade sector <SECTOR>`

## Sectors
IT, Banking/BFSI, Pharma, Auto, FMCG, Metals, Energy, Realty, Infra, Defence, PSU

## What To Do

Sector rotation and momentum analysis for NSE sectors.

## Output Must Include

1. **Sector Trend vs Nifty** — relative strength (outperforming/underperforming)
2. **FII/DII Flow Into Sector** — institutional money direction
3. **Sector-Specific Triggers**:
   - Banking: RBI policy, NPA data, credit growth
   - IT: INR/USD, US tech spending, deal wins
   - Pharma: FDA approvals, USFDA inspections, API prices
   - Auto: monthly sales data, EV transition, input costs
   - FMCG: rural demand, monsoon, raw material costs
   - Metals: commodity prices, China demand, dumping duties
   - Energy: crude oil, gas prices, capex cycle
   - Realty: interest rates, inventory levels, launches
   - Infra: govt capex, order book, execution
   - Defence: order inflows, budget allocation, exports
   - PSU: divestment, dividend yield, re-rating cycle
4. **Top 3 Stocks to BUY** in sector — with entry/stop/target
5. **Top 1 Stock to AVOID/SHORT** — with reason
6. **Options Play** on sector ETF/index (BankNifty, FinNifty, etc.)
7. **Sector Rotation Stage** — early cycle / mid cycle / late cycle / defensive

## Key NSE Sector Indices to Reference
- Nifty Bank, Nifty IT, Nifty Pharma, Nifty Auto, Nifty FMCG
- Nifty Metal, Nifty Energy, Nifty Realty, Nifty Infra, Nifty PSE
- Nifty Media, Nifty Financial Services
