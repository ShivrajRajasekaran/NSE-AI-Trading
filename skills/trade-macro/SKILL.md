# Indian Market Macro View — KARTHIK

## Trigger
`/trade macro`

## What To Do

Full macro picture for Indian markets. Fetch live data via WebSearch.

## Output Must Include

1. **India VIX** — current reading + interpretation
   - <13: Complacency, sell options premium
   - 13-18: Normal, standard strategies
   - 18-25: Elevated fear, reduce position size
   - >25: Panic, avoid selling naked options

2. **Nifty50** — trend, key levels, immediate support/resistance

3. **BankNifty** — trend, key levels (leads Nifty on RBI days)

4. **FII/DII Activity** — rolling 5-day net buy/sell in cash + F&O

5. **INR/USD** — current rate, trend
   - Weak INR = bad for importers (oil, gold), good for IT exporters
   - Strong INR = good for importers, bad for exporters

6. **Brent Crude** — current price, impact
   - High crude = negative for India (import bill), bad for paints, airlines, logistics
   - Low crude = positive, especially for OMCs

7. **10Y Govt Bond Yield** — current, trend
   - Rising yield = bad for NBFC, real estate, long-duration equities
   - Falling yield = positive for rate-sensitive sectors

8. **Global Context**:
   - US Fed stance (hawkish/dovish)
   - DXY (Dollar Index) — strong dollar = FII outflows from India
   - Dow/Nasdaq/S&P direction — overnight impact on gap open
   - China data — impacts metals, chemicals

9. **KARTHIK's Market Regime Call**:
   - BULL / BEAR / CHOP
   - How to trade this regime (trend-follow / mean-revert / sit out)
   - Preferred strategy (directional / non-directional / hedged)

10. **Key Events This Week** — RBI, earnings, global events, expiry
